SISTER PROJECTS

IMPROVING IDEA DISCOVERY AND DEVELOPMENT (I2D2)

The I2D2 project is working to develop and provide new managerial training materials and programs for scientists with the goal of improving coordination, communication, and organization in scientific teams.
